# Getting help

- **A question about the method or a requirement:** [Discussions](https://github.com/RobotRegistryFoundation/bounded-embodiment/discussions), category "Q&A".
- **Something in the method is wrong or missing:** the [Feedback on a requirement or test](https://github.com/RobotRegistryFoundation/bounded-embodiment/issues/new?template=feedback-requirement.yml) form.
- **The kit fails on your machine:** an issue with your OS, Python version, the command you ran and its output.
- **Anything private:** craig@craigmerry.com.

There is one maintainer. Expect a first response within a week.
